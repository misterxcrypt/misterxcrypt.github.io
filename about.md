# About Hariharan M

## In one line
I find what's exposed and who's exploiting it, then build what keeps finding them.

## What I investigate
I work on the part of cybercrime that has infrastructure behind it. A scam is not one
website. It is an advertising estate, a set of look-alike domains, a Telegram channel
recruiting mules, and a chain of accounts moving money. My job is to find that structure,
establish what connects to what and with what confidence, and hand it to the people who
can act on it.

Domains I work across: financial cybercrime and money mule networks, phishing and
typosquat estates, brand and executive impersonation, dark web marketplaces and leak
sites, Telegram ecosystems, and the exposed external attack surface of client estates.

## How I investigate
Passive collection only takes you so far. I engage threat actors directly under
documented OPSEC procedure to verify leaked data, establish modus operandi, and map
infrastructure a collection pipeline never sees. That tradecraft is the difference
between a list of indicators and an understanding of an operation.

Sources and methods: OSINT and HUMINT, sock-puppet and persona operations under SOPs I
authored, Certificate Transparency monitoring, domain and infrastructure intelligence,
infrastructure clustering by hosting and template similarity, adversary TTP mapping to
MITRE ATT&CK, and blockchain tracing of illicit fund flows.

## What I build
The investigation and the engineering are the same job. Every system I have built exists
because a manual process could not run at the rate the problem demanded.

- Collection and correlation across 20+ underground sources, feeding indicators to national fraud-response channels
- Phishing monitoring from Certificate Transparency, typosquatting analysis and domain intelligence, enriched and scored before an analyst sees it
- Internet-scale reconnaissance with ZMap and zgrab2 on Terraform-provisioned AWS
- Telescribe, an open-source Telegram OSINT relationship mapper
- ReconEye, a distributed external attack surface management system
- A WhatsApp-based complaint intake and case-tracking platform for a local cybercrime unit, built on personal time

## What I care about
Intelligence that changes something. The work I am most pleased with is not the largest
dataset. It is the indicators that reached I4C and NPCI, and the complaint system a
cybercrime unit actually uses day to day.

I also care about not overstating. Where a claim on my site cannot be independently
verified, it says so. Independent research is labelled as unaffiliated with any employer,
and stays that way.

## Where I am
Based in India. Experience supporting enterprise, financial and government-focused
security initiatives across India and Southeast Asia.
